Counts are table cells, not features. Partial means it works but with a gap named in the cell.

Solid

Sign-up and email verification for every type, carrier and driver vetting, admin approval, booking through offers or contracts, stop evidence and documents, payment straight to the carrier, factoring and ratings.

Holes

No live GPS, so loads never show In Transit. Nothing for when a load goes wrong: no cancel, edit, disputes, extra charges or claims. Approval isn’t enforced in the API for posting or offers. Brokers don’t exist yet.

Build order

Highest impact on running real loads first.

#BuildWhat it covers
01Driver GPS and trip startSend the driver’s location from My Jobs while a trip is running, and set the load to In Transit when the driver starts the first stop. Unblocks live tracking for shipper, receiver and the public link.
02Cancel and editCancel a load before pickup (release the card hold), edit a posted load, carrier drops a booked load with a reason.
03Lock unapproved companies in the APIBlock posting for unapproved shippers, and the load board and offers for unapproved carriers. Today only the screens hide them.
04DisputesShipper or carrier opens a dispute on a load with evidence, payout is held, admin resolves with a split.
05Extra charges and TONUDetention, layover, lumper, extra stops and truck-ordered-not-used, requested by the carrier and approved by the shipper, added to the charge.
06Contracted load accept, counter-offersCarrier accepts or declines a contracted load; shipper and carrier counter an offer.
07BrokersSign-up, MC and bond check, and what a broker may do on a load. Needs your decision first.
08Damage and shortage claimsNote OS&D at delivery and open a claim against the load.
09Payments: correctionsRefunds and credits from the admin console, real payout request (or remove the button), monthly statements, Plaid secret for bank payments.
10Mobile app, driver firstExpo app: sign-in, invitation, credentials by camera, loads and stops, background GPS, photos, signature, POD, offline queue. Then push notifications with device tokens.
11Mobile app, everyone elseSign-up for every type, carrier dispatch and fleet map, shipper tracking and offers, receiver delivery confirmation, store listings.

Keeping this current

When a piece of work lands, change the cell to Built or Partial and put the commit date and hash on it. Missing cells say Open until a target date is set. Dates show when a cell reached its current state, taken from git history; work before 1 Jun 2026 and anything not committed has no date.
